## Selected direction

Design the interacting ownership contracts together, and bring forward future
capabilities that would otherwise force a near-term structural rewrite. The
assessment's smallest slices are useful commit boundaries, not a ceiling on this
commission. Inherited context, comparison interpretation, explicit work authority,
fact demand and saved selection must agree before their public surfaces harden.

The selected outcome includes:

- A single persisted execution model with partial ancestors, lane-local inherited
  context and exact unstarted decisions; accepted work does not replay on resume.
- Immutable shared selected state, a lower-level owner of semantic validation and
  runtime facts with explicit acquisition/release boundaries.
- Explicit comparator interpretation and proactive installation authority, with
  honest omitted/unknown comparison and selected work independent of physical diff.
- JW command/source inputs shared across receivers; one actual ordinary cohort;
  inherited selected-region policy through paired and opaque traversal.
- Both clear-first and authorized-replacement tracked renewal, chosen before
  mutation and proved against truthful native state. Clear-first is the existing
  control; relative performance will be measured after both routes work.
- Source-only preparation handed into refill, nonmutating `PLANNED` preview,
  phase-aware partial reports, loaded finish and public command cutover.

Native storage, membership and ordinary-selection authority retain their distinct
owners. The outcome claims for D=S and D≠S must be stated independently. Broader
interfaces are justified by coherent use, rather than by a universal registry.
